📌 Key Observations & Product Diagnostic Focus
OBSERVED PATTERN

Mobile conversion (2.01%) is 49.1% lower than Desktop (3.95%), despite representing 59.6% of browsing sessions.

INVESTIGATION

Segmenting by funnel stage reveals that mobile users add to cart at healthy rates (~14%), but abandon disproportionately during multi-step checkout.

HYPOTHESIS

Checkout form complexity and higher page latency on handheld devices are potential contributors requiring controlled experimentation.

🔬 Hypothesis-Driven Investigation Framework
OBSERVED PATTERN

Mobile conversion (2.01%) is 49% lower than Desktop (3.95%); Buy Now Pay Later suffers a 12.07% authorization failure rate.

INVESTIGATION

Cross-tabulating Device x Channel shows Mobile + Paid Social + New Users converts at only 1.80% with 74.8% cart drop-off and 63.8s checkout time.

HYPOTHESIS

Mobile drop-off is associated with checkout input fatigue and payment friction. Slower page load latency is a potential contributor.

RECOMMENDATION

Test a single-page frictionless checkout and deploy an instant card fallback retry modal when BNPL credit checks fail.

SUCCESS METRIC

Mobile Checkout-to-Order Conversion Rate (Target: > 60%) and Payment Authorization Success Rate (> 97%).
